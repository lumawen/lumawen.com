Our mission

Light the way for every player.

We believe a guitar should tell you exactly what you played, and that learning music should feel like having a patient teacher beside you, not a wall of theory.

So we're building the instrument, the pickup and the teacher together, and publishing the hardware for anyone to use.

  • Open by default. Schematics, board files and build guides live on GitHub under open licenses.
  • Made for players. Tools that help you play more and guess less, whether it's your first chord or your thousandth gig.
  • Built to last. Repairable hardware, documented parts, no lock-in.

What we're building

Four pieces, one idea: the guitar and the learning go together.

01 · PICKUPS

Hexaphonic pickups

One pickup per string, six separate signals. Each string can go to its own effect, its own synth voice or straight into the learning app, so it knows exactly which note you played and on which string.

02 · GUITARS

Custom guitars

Instruments designed around the hexaphonic pickup from the start, with clean routing, serviceable electronics and builds tuned to the player who'll own them.

03 · LEARNING

AI-assisted learning app and site

Learn guitar and music theory with a coach that listens. It hears what you play, explains why a chord works, spots the habit that's slowing you down and builds the next lesson around you.

04 · OPEN HARDWARE

Open source hardware

Diagrams, schematics, parts lists and step-by-step build instructions, published on GitHub. Build your own pickup, adapt it to your guitar, and send back what you learn.

Open source

The hardware belongs to everyone who plays.
